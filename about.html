About

Sachin Shivakumar is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Center for Nonlinear Studies (CNLS), Los Alamos National Laboratory. His research focuses on advancing both fundamental theory and computational methods for analysis, estimation, and control of systems governed by partial differential equations (PDEs) and other infinite-dimensional models.

Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University. He completed his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at Arizona State University in 2024 (advisor: Matthew Peet).
